AgentBridge

Human approval for AI agents.

AgentBridge is a browser extension that puts a human approval step between AI agents and your accounts — nothing happens without your explicit OK. It is part of the agent systems YardWork builds for businesses.

Add to Chrome

Why it exists

AI agents can do real work — but you shouldn't let one touch your accounts without a check in the loop. AgentBridge is that check: every sensitive action pauses until you approve it, and every approval is logged.

Features

  • Approval prompts before agent actions
  • Full activity log of what the agent did
  • Works with the agent systems YardWork builds for clients
